## 最终生成提示词

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: one polished Android launcher icon for the Corn family's Modbus client/server (master/slave) simulator and industrial communication app.
Input image 1 is the parent brand reference: preserve its distinctive golden corn mascot, two long black capsule eyes, warm simple kernel seams, soft sage-green leaves, charcoal rounded-square tile, and subtle smooth gradients.
Input image 2 is the sibling Corn Flashing app icon: match its family resemblance, composition, illustration polish and lower-right product badge placement, but invent a NEW communication badge for Modbus. Do not copy its lightning bolt or chip.
Primary request: create a Corn Modbus app icon that clearly suggests bidirectional data exchange and the ability to act as either communication endpoint. Keep the corn dominant, friendly and recognizable. Tucked naturally between the lower leaves, put one compact premium graphite communication badge, occupying approximately the lower-right third. On its face use a very simple large high-contrast communication symbol: two small rounded-square endpoint nodes facing one another, with two bold horizontal arrows between them pointing in opposite directions, one cool turquoise and one warm pale gold. Clean coherent geometry, instantly understandable. Both nodes and the two-way arrows must read as a single balanced emblem, not clutter. The badge is a smooth dark rounded rectangle with a restrained sage rim; no microchip pins, no lightning, no flames. Preserve both eyes unobstructed.
Composition: similar close framing and gentle corn tilt as the parent brand, central visual identity comfortable within Android circular/squircle crops, rounded-square dark tile fills the square canvas, transparent ONLY outside tile corners. This is final icon artwork, not a mockup or collage. Large simple shapes readable at small sizes. Warm gold and sage remain the main colors; turquoise is a small functional communication accent. Soft sculptural gradients, refined clean edges, no excessive glow, no busy traces, no wires, no extra characters. No text, letters, labels, numbers, watermarks, branding words, protocol lettering or decorative border. One single square icon, high resolution.
